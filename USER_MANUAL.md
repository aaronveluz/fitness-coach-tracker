## 2. Dashboard & Coaching Directives

The main **Dashboard** (`/dashboard`) serves as your daily fitness headquarters:
- **Bi-Weekly Meet-Up Banner**: Notifies you of upcoming physical 1-on-1 consultations with Coach Pat and coaching staff.
- **Active Tasks Assigned by Coach Pat**: View prescribed workouts, nutrition goals, and recovery habits assigned specifically to you by Coach Pat. Click **`✓ Complete Task`** once done.
- **Daily Macro Progress**: Real-time calorie and protein burn rings with remaining daily targets.
- **Hydration Tracker**: 1-click water logging (`+250ml`, `+500ml`, `+1000ml`) towards your personalized hydration goal.
- **Recent Activity Feed**: Chronological stream of logged workouts, meals, body weight changes, and coach feedback remarks.

## 3. Food & Daily Nutrition Tracker

The **Food Tracker** (`/food`) provides full macro tracking with custom flexibility:
1. **Logging Meals**:
   - Categorize entries into **Breakfast**, **Lunch**, **Dinner**, or **Snacks**.
   - Search the master database and adjust serving amounts (grams, pieces, scoops) to automatically calculate Calories, Protein, Carbs, and Fats.
2. **Flexible Food & Calorie Database Manager**:
   - Click **`⚙️ Manage Food & Calorie Database`** at the top of the page.
   - **Edit Calories & Macros**: Modify serving sizes, calories, and nutrient profiles for any food item.
   - **Add Custom Foods**: Create custom recipes, local delicacies, and supplements.
   - **Delete Items**: Remove unused items from your library.
3. **Coach Feedback**:
   - Coach Pat reviews your daily nutrition logs and leaves verified feedback remarks on meal adherence.

## 4. Weightlifting Log & 1RM Calculator

The **Weightlifting Tracker** (`/workouts`) records detailed strength progression:
1. **Logging a Workout Session**:
   - Set workout title, split type (*Push, Pull, Legs, Upper, Lower, Full Body*), duration, and energy rating (1–5 stars).
   - Add exercises and track individual sets with **Weight (kg)**, **Reps**, and **RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion 1–10)**.
   - Tag Personal Records (**PR 🏆**).
2. **Interactive 1RM Calculator**:
   - Uses the Epley formula: `1RM = Weight × (1 + Reps / 30)`.
   - Generates working percentage targets (95%, 90%, 85%, 80%, 75%, 70%) for periodized training cycles.
3. **Rest Timer**:
   - Built-in customizable rest countdown timer with sound chime alerts (`60s`, `90s`, `120s`, `180s`).
4. **Coach Verification**:
   - Workouts reviewed by Coach Pat receive an official **`✓ Verified & Approved by Coach Pat`** badge.

## 5. Physique Progress & InBody Composition

The **Physique Tracker** (`/physique`) provides visual and clinical body composition monitoring:
- **Weekly Photo Sets**: Upload and compare Front, Side, and Back progress photos week-over-week.
- **Before & After Interactive Slider**: Drag the visual separator to compare baseline starting photos with your current physique.
- **InBody 570 Clinical Scanner Logs**:
  - Track **Skeletal Muscle Mass (kg)**, **Body Fat Percentage (%)**, **Visceral Fat Level**, and **Total Body Water (%)**.
- **Coach Aesthetic Feedback**: Read Coach Pat's weekly physique critiques and structural symmetry evaluations.

## 6. Body Weight & Trend Tracking

The **Body Weight Tracker** (`/weight`) filters out daily water fluctuations:
- **Daily Weigh-Ins**: Record weight entries with morning/fasted notes.
- **7-Day Moving Average Graph**: Smooth trend curve highlighting true fat loss or lean mass gain over time.
- **Goal Delta & BMI Gauge**: Instant calculations of kilograms remaining to goal weight and clinical BMI range.

## 9. Exercise Library & Custom Exercise Attachments

The **Exercise Library** (`/exercises`) contains verified exercise definitions:
- **Equipment Filtering**: Filter by *Minimal / Home Equipment* (Bodyweight, Dumbbells, Resistance Bands, Pull-up Bar) or *Full Commercial Gym* (Barbell, Cable Machine, Smith Machine, Plate-loaded Machines).
- **Muscle Target Filtering**: Isolate Chest, Back, Shoulders, Quads, Hamstrings, Glutes, Biceps, Triceps, Calves, and Core.
